// 01. EXECUTIVE BRIEFING

Confronting Fraud at Machine Speed

As instant payment rails (FedNow, RTP, SEPA Instant, UPI) achieve global scale, the window between transaction authorization and irrevocable settlement has collapsed to sub-second intervals. Simultaneously, generative AI and automated exploit tooling allow criminal syndicates to orchestrate synthetic identity fraud, deepfake biometric KYC bypasses, and account-to-account (A2A) manipulation at industrialized scale.

The Global Payments & Risk Summit 2027 convenes senior leaders across retail banking, card networks, FinTech platforms, digital commerce merchants, and regulatory authorities to engineer decisive defense mechanisms with zero vendor sales pitches.
