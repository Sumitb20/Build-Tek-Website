AI-Sec Global Summit 2027 — Frequently Asked Questions

It is an intensive 6-hour global virtual summit taking place on 12 February 2027 (08:00 AM – 02:05 PM ET / 13:00 – 19:05 UTC) examining how artificial intelligence is transforming both offensive exploitation and defensive enterprise resilience. Attendance is capped at 200 delegates.

Under the Chatham House Rule, participants are free to use the information received, but neither the identity nor the affiliation of the speaker or any participant may be revealed. This guarantees candid, unvarnished discussion regarding real incident debriefs and security strategy without fear of media attribution.

We enforce a strict no product demos rule. All plenary presentations, teardowns, and reserved partner sessions must focus on technical research, defensive architecture blueprints, and actionable thought leadership. Scripted sales walk-throughs are prohibited.

To ensure high-signal discussions, Standard and Executive Pass registrations are verified against corporate email domains and professional roles (CISOs, VPs, Lead Architects, and researchers). Registrations from commercial vendor sales or marketing teams under practitioner tiers will be asked to upgrade to the Solution Provider Pass or receive a prompt refund.

All registered attendees receive a verified Digital Certificate of Attendance. Formal CPE credit accreditation (for CISSP, CISM, and related security credentials) is currently in application review. Final accreditation details will be confirmed closer to the event date.

Full refunds (less payment processing fees) are available for cancellations requested 30+ days prior to the summit. Between 14 and 29 days prior, a 50% refund is available. The summit will be hosted on an enterprise-grade virtual conferencing platform with dedicated live stream infrastructure, interactive Q&A moderation, and private breakout rooms.
