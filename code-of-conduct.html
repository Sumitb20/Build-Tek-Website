Professional Standards

Code of Conduct & Community Standards

Effective Date: 1 August 2026  |  Last Updated: 9 November 2026

1. Purpose & Guiding Principles

BuildTek Events is committed to providing a safe, respectful, harassment-free, and intellectually rigorous environment for all delegates, speakers, sponsors, and organizers, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, or religion.

2. The Chatham House Rule: Official Framework

Definition: "When a meeting, or part thereof, is held under the Chatham House Rule, participants are free to use the information received, but neither the identity nor the affiliation of the speaker(s), nor that of any other participant, may be revealed."

Application at AI-Sec Global Summit 2027:

  • Closed-Door CISO Roundtable (01:25 – 02:05 PM ET): Strictly governed under the Chatham House Rule. No session recording is captured or archived. Participants may cite insights in their strategic planning but may not attribute statements, statistics, or breach anecdotes to specific individuals or companies.
  • Plenary Masterclasses & Teardowns: Broadcast to registered delegates with attributed speaker presentations. Q&A submissions may be submitted anonymously at the delegate's discretion.

3. Recording, Screenshots & Intellectual Property

To preserve candid discussions and protect enterprise confidentiality:

  • Unauthorized Recording Prohibited: Delegates may not capture screen recordings, audio streams, or raw transcription logs of closed-door sessions.
  • Official Replays: Official high-definition recordings of plenary tracks will be curated and made accessible on the secure delegate portal for 30 days post-event.
  • Social Sharing: General commentary and takeaways on LinkedIn and X using the hashtag #AISec2027 are welcomed, provided they respect the Chatham House Rule and do not expose unredacted proprietary architectures.

4. Expected Professional Behavior

  • Engage with peers and panelists with professional courtesy and constructive critique.
  • Maintain zero commercial solicitation in open Q&A chats. Product pitches are strictly restricted to designated Solution Provider showcases.
  • Respect confidentiality boundaries when discussing incident response workflows or vulnerability timelines.

5. Unacceptable Conduct

  • Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory jokes, or exclusionary language in chat rooms, breakout spaces, or direct messaging.
  • Spamming attendees with unsolicited marketing messages or unauthorized database scraping.
  • Impersonation of delegates, speakers, or organization representatives.

6. Enforcement & Reporting

Participants violating these standards may be expelled from the summit platform without refund at the sole discretion of the event directors. If you experience or witness a violation, please contact our community integrity team immediately at conduct@buildtek.in or alert a platform room moderator.
