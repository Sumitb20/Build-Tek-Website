Event Policy

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 1 August 2026  |  Last Updated: 9 November 2026
Notice: This policy governs delegate registrations for virtual events produced by BuildTek Events, including the AI-Sec Global Summit 2027. All cancellation deadlines are calculated relative to the scheduled broadcast start time (12 February 2027, 08:00 AM ET).

1. Overview

BuildTek Events ("Organizer") produces high-caliber executive assemblies with capped delegate capacity. Because attendance quotas are strictly enforced to preserve peer-to-peer discussions, cancellations directly impact access allocation.

2. Cancellation Windows & Refunds

Requests for cancellations must be submitted in writing via email to registers@buildtek.in from the business email used during registration.

Cancellation Notice Window Refund Percentage Alternative Option
60+ calendar days prior to event date 100% refund (less 10% administrative processing fee) 100% credit transfer to future BuildTek event
30 – 59 calendar days prior to event date 50% refund of total pass fee 100% credit transfer to future BuildTek event or colleague substitution
Less than 30 calendar days prior to event date No cash refund Colleague substitution permitted up to 48 hours prior to broadcast
Event day no-show No refund or credit 30-day on-demand recording portal access remains fully active

3. Delegate Substitutions

If a registered delegate is unable to attend, ticket transfers to a colleague within the same organization are permitted at no additional charge, subject to the following criteria:

  • The substitute must meet the eligibility criteria for the designated pass tier (e.g. Executive Delegate Passes must be assigned to VP/C-Suite leaders).
  • Written notice with the substitute's full name, corporate title, email, and LinkedIn profile must be received by registers@buildtek.in at least 48 hours prior to event commencement.

4. Pass Type Reassignment & Adjustments

In accordance with our Commercial Pass Policy, technology vendors, sales representatives, and consulting service providers registering under the Executive Delegate Pass tier will be contacted by our vetting committee to reassign their registration to the Solution Provider Pass ($999). Should the registrant decline the upgrade, a full refund of the original fee will be processed immediately.

5. Event Rescheduling or Format Changes

In the unlikely event that the summit is rescheduled, postponed, or modified by the Organizer due to technical force majeure or speaker illness, registered passes will automatically transfer to the rescheduled date. If a delegate cannot attend the revised date, a 100% credit or full refund will be provided upon written request.

6. Invoicing & GST Details (India Registrations)

For Indian corporate registrations requesting GST tax invoices, refund calculations will be made on the base registration value excluding non-refundable statutory GST components where credit notes cannot be issued.
